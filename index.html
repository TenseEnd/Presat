05 · Everyday English · Page 36

Jane and Aidar's Saturday

Read the dialogue. What did they do? Did they enjoy it?

Aidar
Hi, Jane! I tried calling you on Saturday night, but you didn't answer your phone.
Jane
Oh, hi Aidar! Yes, sorry! I was at the ballet.
Aidar
Really? What was it like?
Jane
It was fantastic! The dancers were amazing!
Jane
What did you do on Saturday?
Aidar
I stayed home with my brother and we watched a film on TV.
Jane
Did you enjoy it?
Aidar
Not really. It was nothing special. Do you want to go for a walk later?
Jane
Sure!

07 · Across Cultures · Page 37

Festivals around the world

Read the texts and find: when, where and what people do.

United Kingdom 🇬🇧

Shakespeare's Birthday

23 April

Where? Stratford-upon-Avon, the UK.

What happens? A parade, traditional costumes, marching bands, schoolchildren, flowers on Shakespeare's grave, concerts, competitions and dancing.

Kazakhstan 🇰🇿

Festival of Nomadic Civilisation

July

Where? Astana, around the Khan Shatyr shopping centre.

What happens? A mini tent village with yurts, traditional food and crafts, music and dance performances.
